# Content and media provenance

## Client source of truth

`client-materials/website-guideline by client.docx` is the source for the hero copy, KPIs, “why clients partner with us”, company overview, leadership profile, software table, services, training programmes, sector experience and regional offices. The brief additionally states that the business provides **complete fire safety solutions including products and their installation**; this is reflected in the Products pages and the “Supply · Installation · Commissioning” section. The domain and Facebook URL come from `business details.txt`.

The DOCX contains citation artefacts (`JPG + 1`, `PDF`); these are omitted. Outcome language such as “zero-defect handover” is presented as an objective, not a guarantee. Sector experience is attributed to the principal engineer’s professional background, not presented as the LLC’s completed portfolio. No testimonials, client names, awards, phone numbers, street addresses, product brands or specifications were invented. **No phone or WhatsApp number was supplied, so no call/WhatsApp buttons exist** — add them in `src/data/site.ts` once the client provides a number.

The office-address images in the client folder show other companies (Optimus Enviropro, RYSA Infratech); their details are not used.

## Brand artwork

- `public/assets/logo.webp` — the supplied logo, trimmed of excess white margin; proportions and colours unchanged.
- `public/assets/logo-reversed.webp` — reversed variant for dark surfaces (black wordmark → white, red retained, transparent background). Generated by `npm run logo` (`scripts/prepare-logo.cjs`).
- `public/favicon.svg` — simple vortex mark in brand red on charcoal.

## AI-generated imagery (15 images)

Generated server-side by `scripts/images/generate.mjs` using the OpenAI Image API; the key is read from `OPENAI_API_KEY` in `.env` and never reaches the browser. Full-resolution PNG originals and a generation log (model, quality, size, prompt, placement) are in `client-materials/generated/`. Published WebP derivatives (1600 px + 720 px) are in `public/assets/gen/` — 1.9 MB in total for all 30 files.

Routing (configurable in `scripts/images/config.mjs`): **Sunburst** only for the hero and the multi-person installation banner; **Flare** for everything else. **High** quality for the hero, key service and product imagery; **medium** for supporting visuals.
